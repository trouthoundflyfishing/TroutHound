About TroutHound

Free knowledge for anglers who chase trout.

What This Site Is

TroutHound (trouthound.com) is a free, no-nonsense resource for fly fishing and fly tying. The goal is simple: help you catch more trout and tie flies that work — without paywalls, affiliate clutter, or complicated jargon.

Whether you’re picking up a fly rod for the first time or looking for a clean reference on classic patterns, this site is built to be useful on your phone at the river or at the tying desk.

What You’ll Find

  • Fly Fishing — gear basics, casting, reading water, and practical tactics
  • Fly Tying — tools, materials, and core techniques
  • Patterns — proven trout flies with materials lists and tying notes
  • Resources — glossary, tippet chart, and starter recommendations

Philosophy

  • Keep it free and open.
  • Focus on trout and the skills that actually put fish in the net.
  • Respect the resource — ethics and conservation matter.
  • Prefer clear language over marketing.

Contact & Domain

This site is designed to live at trouthound.com. Once you point the domain at your free hosting (GitHub Pages, Netlify, or Vercel), the site is ready to go.

No accounts, no tracking beyond what your host provides, no paid content.
